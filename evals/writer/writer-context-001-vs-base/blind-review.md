# Blind Writer comparison

For each prompt, choose **A** or **B** based only on which output better reflects the writing you want.

## evidence
**Prompt:** Write a concise opening paragraph about why evidence matters in public debate.

### A

In the cacophony of public debate, where emotions often overshadow facts, the importance of evidence cannot be overstated. A robust foundation of factual information is essential for fostering informed discourse and ensuring that decisions are based on solid grounds rather than conjecture or misinformation.

### B

In a world where public debate is increasingly polarized and emotional, the role of evidence cannot be overstated. It serves as the foundation upon which rational discourse is built, providing a structured framework for argumentation and a mechanism for resolving disputes through objective analysis rather than personal opinion or emotional appeal.

## accountability
**Prompt:** Write a concise opening paragraph arguing why public institutions must be judged by outcomes, not promises.

### A

Public institutions must be held accountable not just by the promises they make but by the tangible outcomes they achieve, as these outcomes directly reflect their effectiveness and the trust they deserve from the communities they serve.

### B

Public institutions exist to serve the community, and they must be held accountable not just by the promises they make, but by the tangible outcomes they achieve, as the trust and resources entrusted to them require concrete results that directly impact the well-being of the population they serve.

## power
**Prompt:** Write a concise opening paragraph about how power becomes hardest to see when it is treated as ordinary.

### A

When power is treated as ordinary and integrated into everyday life, it becomes the most difficult to see. The reason being that it operates invisibly, embedded in the fabric of society, in the mundane interactions and structures that govern our lives without us noticing the underlying mechanisms that sustain it.

### B

When power is disguised as ordinary tasks and responsibilities, it becomes the most insidious and difficult to detect, blending seamlessly into the background of daily life and operations, making it nearly invisible to those who do not actively seek it out.
